kind: system-map
---
Harbor Logistics runs an intent-driven outbound motion. A visitor de-anonymizer resolves the company behind a website session, an enrichment agent researches the account on a daily cron, and a two-signal scorer stacks fit and intent into a single 0-100 number. Accounts above 70 are routed to a rep; accounts between 40 and 70 wait for a second intent signal before routing. Every outbound draft passes a human approval gate before it sends. The one part not yet built is the drafting step itself, so a routed account waits in a queue nobody writes against.
